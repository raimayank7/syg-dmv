DMV Practice

States covered

Practice is available for 3 states. Each set of questions is written from that state's own driver handbook.

In the United States a driver license is issued by the state, not by the national government, and each state writes its own traffic laws, its own handbook and its own knowledge test. The agency is called the DMV in many states and has other names in others. The broad rules are alike everywhere, but the numbers a test asks for often are not: a fire hydrant must be cleared by 15 feet in California and Virginia and by 10 feet in Oregon. That is why practice must be state by state.

  • California 30 questions · California Driver's Handbook
  • Virginia 30 questions · Virginia Driver's Manual
  • Oregon 30 questions · Oregon Driver Manual 2026-2027

States not yet covered

We tried to open the handbooks of several other states, among them Texas, New York, Florida, Illinois, Pennsylvania, Michigan, Ohio, Washington and New Jersey. Their sites refused the download or the file could not be read by our tools, so we have written nothing for them. For those states, get the handbook from the official licensing agency. The United States government's directory at usa.gov lists each state's motor vehicle agency.

Questions people ask

Which states are covered?

California, Virginia and Oregon. We add a state only after reading its handbook.

Why is my state missing?

Its official site did not allow the handbook to be downloaded when we tried, or we have not reached it yet. Use your state's own handbook and any practice test on its official site.

Are driving rules the same in every state?

No. Many numbers differ, such as speed limits without signs and parking distances. Study your own state.

Independent practice site. DMV Practice is not a government website and is not connected with any Department of Motor Vehicles or licensing agency. The questions are our own, written from each state's public driver handbook as we read it on 12 November 2026. They are not the official test questions. Laws change: the current handbook of your own state is the authority.
